About Metronova

Engineering hardware with a purpose.

We're an advanced-hardware group headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha — built to design, manufacture and support the hardware that pharma, energy, automotive, medical, marine and industrial systems depend on.

Who we are

A hardware group with a wide brief, and a single standard.

Metronova Scintific Research Private Limited designs, manufactures, assembles, distributes and markets hardware products and solutions across a broad range of industries — including pharmaceuticals, automotive, medical, construction, fishing, energy and industrial sectors, and more. We integrate hardware with software, IoT and AI to make the systems built on top of it safer, more efficient and more productive.

What ties it together isn't a single product line — it's a single standard: hardware that is engineered properly, manufactured responsibly, and supported long after it ships.

Vision

To be the most preferred and trusted manufacturing and solutions partner — for every industry whose systems depend on hardware built right.

Mission

To engineer reliable, sustainable and locally-relevant hardware — from first prototype to after-sales support — across the industries that keep our country running.

Our charter

Eight objectives. One standard.

This is the founding charter behind every project we take on — the objectives that define what Metronova is built to do, in the order we set them.

01

Design, Manufacture & Market Hardware

We design, manufacture, assemble, distribute and market hardware products and solutions for a wide range of industries — including pharmaceuticals, automotive, medical, construction, fishing, energy and industrial sectors, and many more.

02

Research, Development & Innovation

We engage in research, development, innovation and technological advancement related to hardware products and systems — offering cutting-edge and reliable solutions that meet the needs and aspirations of our customers and stakeholders.

03

Integrated Hardware & Software Systems

We provide hardware components, devices, equipment and systems that integrate with software and other technologies to enhance operational efficiency, safety and productivity — across pharmaceuticals, automation, medicine, fishing, energy, artificial intelligence, renewable energy and more.

04

Customized Industrial Solutions

We're committed to providing customized hardware solutions that meet the specific requirements of each industry we serve — solutions we believe are necessary to make a real difference to our country and to society at large.

05

Consultancy & Engineering Services

We offer consultancy and engineering services in the design, integration and optimization of hardware systems and components for diverse industry applications, ensuring the highest standards of performance and quality.

06

Manufacturing Facilities & Sustainable Production

We establish and operate manufacturing facilities, production plants and assembly lines for the creation of innovative hardware products — ensuring scalability, efficiency and environmental sustainability at every stage.

07

Strategic Collaboration & Partnerships

We collaborate with business leaders, partners, research institutions and governments across multiple channels to advance technological innovation in hardware design, production and implementation throughout the country.

08

After-Sales Service & Support

We're committed to fulfilling every objective above through dependable after-sales service, technical support and training in relation to our hardware products and technologies.

Our values

What the charter looks like in practice.

Quality & Reliability

Engineering discipline and rigorous testing, so the hardware performs long after the warranty conversation is over.

Innovation

Continuous R&D that keeps our designs current with where each industry is actually heading.

Sustainability

Collaboration

Working alongside industry leaders, research institutions and government to move faster, together.

How we work

From first sketch to the support call.

A genuine sequence, not a decoration — this is the path every Metronova hardware program follows.

R&D & Discovery

Understand the operating environment, constraints and real requirement behind the request.

Design & Engineering

Architect the hardware and its software/IoT integration points, then validate the approach.

Manufacturing & Assembly

Build in facilities engineered for scalable, sustainable production.

Integration & Testing

Verify performance, safety and productivity gains under real operating conditions.

Delivery & After-Sales

Deploy, then stay — with technical support, servicing and training.
